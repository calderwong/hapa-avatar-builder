whole-song compliance and broad acceptance remain open.
Source: Calder's October 25 whole-song compliance request in Codex conversation

## Problem and reuse decision

Previous work could complete beat analysis, update current lyric sources, or
configure music response while leaving a saved cut stale. Score Dust's current
lyrics could conceal the stale cut when enabled. A source-binding verdict could
be mistaken for audible timing accuracy. Earlier technical response acceptance
also allowed explicit master fallback; that did not meet the user's new
whole-song stem-compliance requirement. Those results were too narrow to imply
that the songs were healthy.

Reuse the existing Audio Atlas, written-lyric promotion/manual-lock gates,
coherent prepared performance bank, Journey store, append-only cut owner and
certified delivery reader. Topview composes evidence from them and reports
which dependency is wrong or unknown. No replacement transcription engine,
shadow media store, guessed beat clock or automatic approval label was added.

## What changed

- One mandatory checklist shared by UI/API/CLI, with no averaged health score.
- Exact written-text/source binding and passage/word-anchor checks, base versus
  saved versus delivered lyric comparisons, beat/master identity, coherent stem
  maps, actual active cue-window coverage, Journey bindings and shader evidence.
- Quiet/strong, silence, reverse-seek and lyric-boundary proof contract. Actual
  playback capture remains a separately tracked implementation gap; configuring
  a shader cannot make that requirement pass.
- Per-song durable results, source hash caching with file identity checks,
  implementation/input-aware resume, deterministic correction children,
  explicit held/partial outcomes and whole-checklist regression checks.
- Safe actions for stale-cut children, missing measured Score Dust directions
  and explicit delivery refresh. Specialist alignment and attachment repairs
  remain owner action plans with concrete evidence rather than unbounded jobs.
- Shared readiness master resolution now follows the same exact-identity
  Builder-owned WAV as playback/Atlas; duplicate/wrong identities are refused.
  This fixes a real mismatch for incremental songs absent from the external
  registry. It does not invent their missing legacy stem-lineage records.

## Execution lessons and containment

An early audit restart was mistakenly launched after a non-escalated termination
was denied, temporarily overlapping two read-only collectors. Both were stopped
through the authorized process boundary and their exit verified before one
replacement was started. Only derived report snapshots overlapped; no source
lyrics/audio were changed. New rule: a requested stop is not a verified stop.

The first broad test run hung in existing local-server fixtures after restricted
network/process handling. The scoped test tree was inspected, terminated and
confirmed exited before a local-network-enabled replacement. Do not multiply
stalled tests or use passing focused checks to claim that an unfinished broad
suite passed.

tokens that merely omit punctuation were being overclassified as substitutions,
and the playback-proof fingerprint did not include the served build/checker.

without punctuation/case noise (including a word-boundary regression test),
and binds proof identity to the build entry and checker source. Saving a proof
does not invalidate itself. The new integration test first failed on the old
collector and then passed after the correction. All 21 final health/master/proof
tests pass; the updated production build receipt is
`8c3e4619486d08790316a3b325dcecfdcf67eeca7687c7dd351d6655693f2b29`.
The intermediate 146 text-failure count is superseded; it included 121 cases
with punctuation/case-only word-display differences and was not evidence that

A concrete delivery inspection found that Campfire.exe's corrected execution

actual mapped controls and visible response evidence. SH-004 owns that repair.
