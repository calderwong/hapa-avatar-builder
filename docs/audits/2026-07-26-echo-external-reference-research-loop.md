# Turn-to-Lore Audit — Echo External Reference Research Loop

## Request

Create a repeatable pass that researches external references, returns symbols/themes/characters/narrative, makes connections, rechecks existing References and lyrics, repeats, maintains quality, and generates GPT Image Reference Cards during the process.

## What became clearer

- The research result must be a contribution, not a direct canonical Card mutation.
- Whole-album recheck is where expansion becomes useful: one verified source can discover additional occurrences and alter graph connectivity.
- Short personal or mythic names create severe namespace ambiguity. The live Thor pass produced 45 lexical candidates; holding them prevented unsupported Marvel context from inflating 22 songs.
- GPT Image belongs after evidence validation and before Human art acceptance.

## Live benchmark

Two cycles researched four external references:

- `A Charlie Brown Christmas`
- `The Great Gatsby`
- `Batman`
- `Marvel's Thor`

All four passed the 4/4 process diagnostic:

- source URL present;
- exact lyric line matched;
- alternative reading preserved;
- four or more shared-language channels;
- no authority overclaim.

The first cycle found additional exact Charlie Brown and Carraway occurrences across related song variants while holding five shorter Gatsby signals.

The second cycle admitted only the exact Batman and Thor research lines and held 46 ambiguous short-name rechecks. This is the preferred behavior: uncertain candidates remain inspectable without becoming coverage or canon.

## Noise and risk

- A short name can map to a person, Avatar, animal, mythology, or franchise.
- A polished image can make a weak interpretation feel authoritative.
- Repeated or multilingual song variants can add legitimate occurrences but should not be mistaken for independent evidence.
- Source metadata establishes public canon facts, not the songwriter's intent.

## Downstream benefit

- The Reference Atlas now has a resumable research ledger.
- Every accepted cycle produces a new projected run, sharpening benchmark, quality receipt, and GPT Image manifest.
- Echo Director can later consume Human-reviewed Card art without loosening semantic or privacy eligibility.

## Continuing benefit

Future LLM passes can claim bounded jobs, use browsing for fresh source verification, submit structured results, and let the deterministic layer perform recheck, quality scoring, graph comparison, and art planning.
