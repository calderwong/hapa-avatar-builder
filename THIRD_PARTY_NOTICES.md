## pallet-town-3d procedural design systems

The Echo Record Garden visual system adapts general-purpose procedural-noise,
texture-baking, material-family, foliage-layout, atmosphere, and scene-dressing
techniques from the MIT-licensed `pallet-town-3d` project.

- Source: https://github.com/PauliusOS/pallet-town-3d
- Source revision: `d49e0c0de836892133b3b21f50c9d29749879db5`
- Upstream files consulted or adapted:
  - `src/core/Noise.ts`
  - `src/core/TextureLab.ts`
  - `src/fx/BuildingMaterials.ts`
  - `src/fx/Clouds.ts`
  - `src/fx/FoliageMaterials.ts`
  - `src/fx/PropMaterials.ts`
  - `src/world/Atmosphere.ts`
  - `src/world/Buildings.ts`
  - `src/world/Props.ts`
  - `src/world/Vegetation.ts`
- Local adaptations:
  - `src/components/echo-record-garden-noise.js`
  - `src/components/echo-record-garden-visual-system.js`

Only generic, franchise-neutral procedural techniques are used. No upstream
character, creature, dialogue, logo, franchise name, recognizable town layout,
or binary art asset is included in the Record Garden.
